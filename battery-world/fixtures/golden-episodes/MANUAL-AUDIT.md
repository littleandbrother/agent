# B0-000 manual semantic audit

Audit target: the deterministic suite generated by
`scripts/build_golden_episodes.py`.

- G0-PASS: the diagnosis is correct, the receiver ACK precedes the deadline,
  voltage stays within bounds, static resources fit, and PMP/EA/VMS all pass.
- G1-OVERVOLTAGE: receiver delivery and diagnostic quality pass, but observed
  3.8 V exceeds the visible 3.6 V contract, so electrical safety and PMP fail.
- G2-BROWNOUT: total harvested and task energy match G0, but the TX-aligned
  voltage reaches 1.7 V, the MCU browns out, and no receiver ACK exists.
- G3-WRONG-DIAGNOSIS: voltage and receiver delivery pass before the deadline,
  but `normal` conflicts with the `bearing-fault` label, so quality and PMP fail.
- G1-G3 are intentionally admitted by the incomplete public predictor; each is
  counted as false admission and rejected by sealed mission truth.
- All displayed properties have source channel, transform, unit, source kind,
  fidelity, and episode provenance.
- Failure explanations remain hypotheses; none is marked causally confirmed.

Result: semantic cases accepted for machine sealing. This audit does not accept
the fixtures as formal Agent, hardware, solver-calibration, or RSI evidence.
