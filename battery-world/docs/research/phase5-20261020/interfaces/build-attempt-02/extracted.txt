     Information, Qualified Observations and Event-Time

                                                Abstract
     Battery-free design decisions depend on when energy becomes usable, which state survives a reset
     and what a receiver must learn. A representation that explains one successful trajectory can
     omit the mechanism activated by the next legal design change. We formalize this distinction as
     current-verdict versus intervention sufficiency, give an elementary factorization condition, and
     exhibit ideal storage-clamp and reset-memory counterexamples. We then implement a bounded
     route from acquired source data through independently qualified, typed observations to actual
     design consumers. A matched comparison uses four fixed digital tasks and three formal repetitions
     per condition. Average-energy inputs yield 8/12 complete-service passes; rich-static, complete-
     input, executable-response and equal-information table inputs each yield 12/12. Prospective live
     observation yields seven completed chains and five incomplete groups, retaining all twelve in the
     denominator. Six completions select supplied fields and one constructs a relation; none supplies a
     physical measurement. A secondary event-service study separates event matching from timestamp
     accuracy: two one-second designs match all eight events and pass energy yet fail encoded-time
     bounds at 1.004 and 1.103 s. Three 0.75-second designs pass; four other episodes terminate without
     a submission. These results identify information and admission boundaries without demonstrating
     superiority over complete static information. Physical qualification remains UNKNOWN, and
     prior product failures remain unaccepted.




1   Introduction
A battery-free device must turn an intermittent supply into a particular service: capture an external event,
preserve the relevant state, and deliver the required information at the required time. Having enough energy
in total does not specify whether a rail becomes ready before a deadline, whether a transmit pulse causes a
reset, or whether the event is still pending when the device recovers. The interface presented to a designer
determines which of these relations can be evaluated before a legal change is made.
The distinction matters even when the current service succeeds. Two worlds can produce the same present
voltage and event traces while having different unexcited storage clamps. Reducing capacitance activates
those clamps and separates their outcomes. Two pending-event memories can likewise behave identically until
a reset is introduced. These examples do not imply that static descriptions are intrinsically inadequate. A
static description of the relevant laws, parameters, initial state and legal intervention domain can already
answer the prospective question [4, 5].
An observation interface offers another route: acquire a source, select or construct a relation, qualify its
domain and units, and feed the result into a design consumer. That route has an operational burden of its


                                                     1
own. A useful equation with an invalid path can fail admission; a correctly computed Boolean can fail to
match a numeric consumer branch. A qualified observation can also leave the final design unchanged, or
support a design that complete static information already permits. Source acquisition, typed consumption,
service success and incremental benefit therefore require distinct evidence [6, 7, 12].
We study these distinctions with sealed, executable digital BF cases. The first part states the information
requirement and instantiates two structural counterexamples. The second implements the acquisition-to-
consumer route and compares it against average-energy, rich-static, complete-input and executable interfaces
under matched budgets. The main formal comparison has four fixed tasks and three repetitions per condition.
Its strongest negative result is central: rich-static information already obtains 12/12 complete-service passes,
whereas live observation completes seven chains and leaves five groups incomplete. The equal-information
serialization comparison gives the same 12/12 service and 65 requests in both arms [11, 12].
A secondary vibration-event study tests a different service boundary. Correct event matching and energy
accounting do not ensure correct encoded event times. Two designs fail one-second timestamp tolerances even
though their messages arrive promptly. We retain these submitted failures separately from four token-censored
episodes with no design submission [18].
The contribution is a connected account of what information must distinguish, how an observation must reach
a real consumer, and where successful digital checks stop supporting the claim. It includes the theoretical
condition, mechanism witnesses, strong static controls, actual failed and completed observation chains,
and portable saved-data reconstruction. It supplies no claim of priority over general sufficient-statistic or
abstraction theory, no advantage over the successful complete static controls, and no hardware qualification.
The study concerns public development apparatus rather than an official benchmark or a population of unseen
physical devices.

2     Related Work
Intermittent systems research addresses the consistency of execution across power failures. Alpaca organizes
a program into tasks, privatizes shared data and commits task effects so interrupted execution can restart
without exposing partial updates [1]. This establishes a concrete recovery mechanism; it does not make a
current energy summary sufficient for a different capacitor or establish that an event reached its receiver. Our
retained-state witnesses use an idealized latch and do not implement or evaluate Alpaca.
Curricle specifies intermittent idempotence requirements through a type system and information-flow qualifiers,
checking programs against those requirements [2]. Its program-level consistency question complements our
admission question: whether an observation has declared units and domain, is independently qualified on the
supplied source, and reaches a compatible design consumer. Passing these interface checks does not prove
intermittent idempotence, arbitrary program correctness or physical validity.
Counterexample-guided abstraction refinement (CEGAR) refines an abstraction when an abstract counterex-
ample is spurious in the concrete system [3]. Our formulation shares the use of distinguishability to explain
missing information. Its role is narrower: two legal digital worlds with the same representation require
different verdicts after a specified intervention. Proposition 1 is an elementary factorization condition, not
a new general theory of abstraction or a CEGAR algorithm. We supply neither a general model checker
nor an automated refinement guarantee. The empirical contribution is the source-bound comparison among
complete static information, executable feedback and qualified live observations, including failed chains and
receiver-side service conditions.

3     The information required by a design consumer

3.1    Current verdicts and legal interventions
Let T fix the external service and its evaluator, w ∈ W denote a world including device laws and state rules,
and d0 be a current design. The complete nominal verdict is VT (w, d) ∈ {PASS, FAIL}. The representation
Rd0 (w) is the information legally available at the current design. It may contain parameters, records, source


                                                       2
definitions or traces; it need not be a scalar. A legal intervention δ produces δ(d0 ). Comparisons below use
the same T and only interventions jointly legal in the compared worlds. A change in the success criterion is
not a design intervention.
Current-verdict sufficiency requires

                                Rd0 (w) = Rd0 (w′ ) =⇒ VT (w, d0 ) = VT (w′ , d0 ).                                (1)

Intervention sufficiency on a declared common domain D requires the stronger implication

                    Rd0 (w) = Rd0 (w′ ) =⇒ VT (w, δ(d0 )) = VT (w′ , δ(d0 ))    for every δ ∈ D.                   (2)

Both are properties of a representation, consumer, world domain and task together. A field set sufficient for
energy balance need not be sufficient for event retention or timestamp accuracy. Nor does either property
promise that a finite-budget language-model procedure will use the information well.
Proposition 1 (Elementary factorization condition). On a fixed common legal domain, an exact prospective
predictor g(Rd0 (w), δ) = VT (w, δ(d0 )) exists if and only if the verdict for each δ is constant on every equivalence
class of worlds sharing Rd0 .

Proof. If g exists, identical representations and the same intervention give it identical arguments, so their
required verdicts must agree. Conversely, if the verdict is constant on each class, define g by that class’s
verdict for the requested intervention. Constancy makes the definition independent of the representative
world. This is an information condition; the proof does not establish an efficient implementation or calibrated
world model.

An interface can return UNKNOWN when its equivalence class contains both verdicts. Abstention then
differs from an incorrect PASS or FAIL. We also distinguish this epistemic answer from INCOMPLETE, the
operational outcome when an episode supplies no admitted final execution. Physical qualification UNKNOWN
is a third boundary: it describes absent calibration evidence even when the digital verdict is well defined.

3.2   Refinement and the strong static alternative
Acquiring an observation O refines the representation to (R, O). It is informationally useful for a particular
intervention only if it separates worlds whose prospective verdicts were previously ambiguous, or supplies
another needed input to the consumer. Provenance, domain and unit checks establish admissibility of the
value. They do not, by themselves, establish that the value distinguishes the relevant worlds or changes
service.
A complete static model is an important limiting case. If its supplied parameters, laws and initial state
determine the execution for every legal d, then executing or reasoning about that model defines an exact g on
the stated ideal domain. A dynamic response can make a computation easier to inspect, and an observation
can reduce the information a consumer must handle. Neither has an automatic informational advantage over
that complete static model. A finite-budget comparison must measure any computational or operational
advantage rather than infer it from the word “executable.”
This separates three interface families evaluated here. A current trace describes one realized behavior. A
declarative static model additionally specifies how permitted changes affect that behavior. An executable
response computes candidate-specific consequences. A qualified observation is a source-bound intermediate
fact delivered through a typed edge. These are distinct interfaces, but their useful information can overlap
completely. The static-grid checks and matched rich-static arm explicitly test that overlap [4, 12].

3.3   Service has several independent predicates
The BF service consumer combines energy feasibility, event capture, retained state, message validity and
timing. An ordered retention witness requires the same event to be captured before a reset and first validly
received afterward. A reset after delivery does not satisfy that witness. Similarly, matching an event identifier


                                                          3
does not establish a correct encoded event time. For a confirmed event at t∗ , receipt at trx and encoded stamp
t̂, an O-style service includes both

                                      0 ≤ trx − t∗ ≤ L,      |t̂ − t∗ | ≤ ϵ,                                (3)

in addition to energy and state predicates. The later experiment uses L = 10 s and ϵ = 1 s. The receive
clock and the encoded stamp are different quantities. Passing the first inequality cannot replace checking the
second [17, 18].

4     Mechanism counterexamples and their sufficient conditions

4.1    An unexcited storage clamp: E2
Two ideal worlds receive 20 µW for 2 s and face a 30 µJ service transaction at 2.5 s. Both start with zero
stored energy, use C = 10 µF and must retain the energy

5 V, all 40 µJ is retained and the margin is 8 µJ, so service passes. The task, source, requested transaction

prospective verdict [4].

4.2    An unexcited reset transition: E3
The second pair captures event 1 at 1 s and attempts its single transmission at 4 s, before a 5 s deadline. A
volatile pending memory and a retained pending memory both deliver in the current uninterrupted execution.
The same legal change inserts a power outage from 2 to 3 s. Volatile memory clears the pending event and
delivers none; retained memory delivers one. External event timing and the transmission schedule remain
fixed. This witness changes a state transition rather than scaling an energy total [4].
The sufficient static state description must state capture, reset and send semantics and their order. If pending
state survives each intervening reset, and the scheduled send can execute after restoration, the captured event
remains available. If the only pending copy is cleared and no recapture is possible before sending, it does not.
These are conditions of the ideal machine, not a claim that an arbitrary nonvolatile device has free writes,
atomic persistence or a qualified energy budget.
The saved analytic and numerical clamp implementations and the separate memory interpreter agree on
eight current/intervention cases at both 1 ms and 0.5 ms settings: sixteen resolution-specific comparisons.
Maximum reported energy discrepancy is 2.84 × 10−12 µJ. The enriched static model agrees on all 28 exposed
grid points, and an additive static control agrees on five service costs spanning PASS and FAIL. Removing the
unexcited clamp or reset-retention fact restores aliasing in these encodings. These checks establish software
consistency under common ideal assumptions, not independent physical truth or universal necessity of a
particular field name [4].

5     From a missing relation to an executed consumer

5.1    A bounded observation language and external qualification
The observation interface makes the information path explicit (Figure 1). The participant can list and acquire
permitted sources, propose either a selected field or a constructed relation, and submit a typed design policy.
A proposal states the source identifier, JSON Pointer paths, units, applicability domain and intended relation.
Selecting a supplied field and constructing a new expression are recorded as different operations. Neither
label alone establishes a new physical fact or a novel scientific relation [6, 9].

Construction uses a bounded JSON abstract syntax tree rather than arbitrary Python. The interpreter admits
at most 64 nodes, depth eight, an 8 MB JSON source and 32,768 vector elements. A construction must
contain a source field and an allowed operator. Unit dimensions must agree across operations; the declared
output unit must match the root, and declared numeric domain constraints are checked against source values.
Missing fields, unsupported domains and invalid arithmetic lead to rejection or UNKNOWN. These limits
bound interpretation; they do not calibrate the supplied source.
Authority is outside the proposer. The host binds the original source bytes, proposal code and input,
provider/run identity and actual accounting. An independent interpreter checks the source relation. A
proposer cannot qualify its own output by supplying an authority record or a success label. The consumer
receives only an admitted observation and the original policy, then chooses among at most three strictly
typed equality branches. Boolean true and a numeric duration are different values. The complete selected
input, observation edge and actual execution identity are preserved before the separate service verdict is
read [6, 8, 9].
This design distinguishes acquisition from consumption. Echoing a value in a report is insufficient. A valid
consumption receipt shows how that value entered the executed design. It still does not establish causal
necessity: the same design might be chosen with complete static inputs, and an observation can be consumed
without changing the design. A claimed benefit would require a separately frozen comparison of alternatives
with the same task and allowance.

6     Matched digital experiment

6.1    Tasks, outcome and strong controls
The main comparison uses four fixed tasks. Pulse-fast is a static-sufficient control. Volatile-reset and
latched-reset use the transient BF apparatus with different pending-state semantics. Their legal design
variables are capacitance and earliest permitted transmission, under frozen task-specific ranges. Complete
service uses independent energy and receiver checks plus the registered ordered-event analysis. PASS denotes
admitted complete service, FAIL an admitted final design that fails it, and INCOMPLETE no admitted final
execution [10, 11].
The fourth task is a preregistered C-lite single-query derivative. It supplies 20 µW ideal DC for 30 s, 1 µW
idle load, zero initial energy and a 50 µJ reply. A query at 2 s must receive its matching reply by 7 s with
maximum data age 5 s. Capacitance is 25–100 µF at an ideal 2 V ceiling; ESR, boot energy and boot delay
are zero and harvest efficiency is one. Gates require query issuance, capture, matching token, causal order,
deadline and freshness. The fixed token value 1 represents one virtual query, not a secure multi-query protocol
or an RF environment. These are explicit synthetic assumptions [15].
Average-energy begins with the coarse source integral and average power. Rich-static and complete-input supply
all input records and the full model definition. They have the same available facts in this registered comparison,
making their tie an admissible control. The saved condition name for complete-input is complete-raw; it does
not mean that the participant receives the full coupled physical trace. That trace is host-side audit material.
Executable-response adds candidate-specific public energy ledgers, activity and packets. Live E5 begins
coarse and actually acquires permitted records, proposes a field or relation, obtains external qualification and
submits a typed consumer policy. No target expression, required-field list or answer-bearing relevance label is
prefilled [10, 12].
E4 changes only the rich-static initial serialization, comparing JSON with a typed JSON-Pointer table. The
table preserves nested containers, types and empty values. All twelve formal pairs have saved receipts of
identical decoded initial packets and tools. Later source responses use common JSON in both arms. This
is an initial-serialization comparison rather than a representation intervention across the whole interactive
trajectory.

6.2    Repetitions, resource parity and cache exposure
Two pilot repetitions on volatile-reset precede the formal comparison. The registered one-time instability rule
selects three formal repetitions, then freezes that count. Pilots remain separate development evidence. The
full eight-condition matrix contains 112 unique groups and 140 actual episodes: 96 formal groups and 16 pilot
groups. Parent method proposals account for additional episodes in two conditions. This paper displays six

interface conditions, covering 72 formal and 12 pilot groups. Rich-static is also E4 JSON, and complete-input
is also the fixed-method cell; these shared cells are counted once [10, 11].
Within each compared task, the scientific laws, service evaluator, legal design domain, reported model alias,
initial cache and total resource caps remain fixed. Information access varies as declared by the condition.
Each group has 24 provider requests, 40 tool calls, 1,200 s and at most two distinct design exposures, counting
checks, response queries and confirmation. Acquisition, qualification and consumption do not add a free
E5 budget. A parent proposal, when present, has an eight-request, 400 s sublimit within the group. The
campaign caps are 3,000 requests, USD 200 in settled conservative estimates and 134,400 summed episode
seconds. Failed groups are not automatically retried or replaced.
Every group starts from one immutable cache containing twelve exact-input responses and one registered
derivative baseline. Private overlays isolate new job histories. A cache hit still consumes logical design
exposure; it does not reveal another group’s output before a query or enlarge the two-design cap. The records’
term “physical cache” denotes the underlying digital execution cache and supplies no laboratory evidence.
Fresh participant entries and the common cache identity are retained per group. The model is reported as
deepseek-flash; this alias does not establish a pinned weight identity [11].

6.3    Preparation and negative controls
The structural preparation separately exports 64 inputs across four cases, four information levels, two formats
and two observation regimes. Thirty-two format pairs preserve decoded facts, assets, tools and resource
limits. These are preparation checks, distinct from actual model trials. Fixed parser checks abstain on all
four current-only cases and correctly judge all four sufficient-information cases in both supplied serializations.
They do not measure language-model comprehension [4, 6].
Negative controls retain the enriched static solution, complete raw model, uninformative observation, exhausted
acquisition budget, illegal design changes and tampered source binding. A researcher-specified required-field
selector and a researcher-supplied headroom expression are construction baselines, not autonomous discoveries.
Preparation outcomes are not substituted for later live completion, and software validation of a consumer
edge is not promoted to hardware acceptance.

neither pending-state survival across a reset nor the necessity of the acquired observation. New physical

8.1    The original O service and its strong fixed control

assumptions [17].
Start requires RMS continuously above 0.05 g for 3 s; stop requires RMS continuously below 0.01 g for 60 s.
Equality resets confirmation. The confirmation instant is the event time. Each start and stop must arrive
within 10 s and carry an encoded timestamp within 1 s; active keepalive gaps must not exceed 120 s and idle
false events fail. The first dark interval contains one required active interval, so darkness cannot simply be
excluded from service.

charged at every poll. These proposed costs do not qualify a vibration sensor, PMIC, clock, NVM or RF

9     Discussion and limitations

9.1    What changes when information is sufficient
The structural witnesses establish a precise failure of the current representation: identical present behavior
can coexist with distinct responses to the same legal change. The enriched static controls show that the failure
is not a universal static-versus-dynamic divide. Once the relevant law and state transition are supplied, the
ideal tasks admit static prospective judgments. The matched comparison then tests actual finite-budget use,
where the complete static arms already saturate service.
Live acquisition introduces more than information content. Source identity, source-local unit maps, domains,
typed policy branches, proposal caps and final submission all become operational gates. The seven prospective
E5 completions establish that this route can reach an actual consumer. Its five incomplete groups expose
the cost of failing before execution. A compiler accepting a relation, an independent scalar agreement, a
changed design and complete service are useful but different observations. None is automatically evidence of a
necessary new fact or benefit over a sufficient static baseline.
The O result further narrows what the consumer must check. A representation containing only matched
counts and energy PASS would omit the distinction that separates the two one-second failures from complete
event service. Adding receive delay alone would still miss the encoded-time failure. The relevant interface
must expose the predicate’s actual operands. This is a concrete consumer-specific information requirement,
not a blanket request for a larger trace.

9.2    Limits on generalization and acceptance
The four main tasks are fixed public development inputs with three formal repetitions, not a random sample
of task families. Pilot evidence and formal evidence remain separate. The same reported model alias is used,
but pinned weight identity is UNKNOWN. Source and evaluator hashes support execution identity; they
cannot recover unavailable weight identity or establish an invoice audit. The current study does not estimate
statistical significance, format equivalence, general interface superiority or stable recursive improvement.
The ideal clamp and memory models share assumptions across their independent implementations. The
transient source is prescribed, the passive latch is ideal, and the C-lite source has no measured RF conversion
or load response. O adds proposed RMS costs and a modeled clock, not measured sensor, PMIC, NVM
or PHY behavior. Range consistency, conservation checks, software correctness and physical qualification
therefore remain distinct. A digital PASS does not qualify source spectra, startup transients, leakage, clock
uncertainty, write persistence or radio reliability.
Original A/R dark-report and strict FIFO failures remain preserved, with product status NOT_ACCEPTED. O
event success neither erases those obligations nor accepts a complete product [22]. Likewise, avoiding a reset
is not retaining state through one, and matching an event is not encoding its time correctly. These scope
distinctions prevent an attractive sub-result from answering a broader untested question.

10    Saved-data reproducibility and implementation handoff
The accompanying E_reproduce.py uses only the Python standard library. Given –data-root data and
–output generated, it verifies the portable input manifest and rebuilds E-series CSVs, all eleven numerical
tables, counts, timestamp differences and preservation checks. It reads only package-relative saved files.
Absolute paths inside historical records are archival strings and are never opened as fallback inputs. Missing
or tampered data fails the check rather than dropping a group. No archived scientific Python is imported,
and no simulator, observation qualifier, consumer or evaluator is rerun.
The package includes a data dictionary, claim-to-evidence index, case/observation implementation handoff and
a model/version, exposure, intervention and cost index. Original syntax failures, post-hoc labels, incomplete
groups, old UNKNOWN records’ accounting boundary and product failures remain explicit. The shared
official HKUST(GZ) style and vector mark are copied unchanged. Installed TeX and Poppler build and
render the standalone manuscript; the document receipt records two-pass compilation, embedded fonts and
actual per-page visual inspection. Saved-data reconstruction and document validation do not constitute a
new scientific replication, fresh hardware test or external submission review.

11    Conclusion
An interface supports a legal design change when it distinguishes the world differences relevant to that
consumer’s prospective verdict. Current success need not provide that information, while complete static
laws can. In the matched digital comparison, strong static and executable inputs each pass all twelve formal
groups; prospective observation completes seven and leaves five incomplete. Actual typed consumption is
demonstrated, but an advantage over complete static information is not. The event-time study shows a further
boundary: correct matching and energy can coexist with wrong encoded timestamps. The resulting evidence
supports explicit information, admission and service predicates, with failures, costs and UNKNOWN retained.
Physical qualification remains UNKNOWN, the original product remains NOT_ACCEPTED, and default H0
remains unchanged.
