                              Model-Authored Improvers
                               for Battery-Free Design

                                                 Working draft

                             Phase 4 local experimental report, 20 October 2026




                                                   Abstract
      Can changing a method-proposal procedure improve battery-free design under a fixed scientific
      task and a complete experimental cost boundary? We compare ordinary reflection with one
      model-authored replacement using three paired descendant lineages, a shared historical packet, and
      fresh design episodes. All 30 effective selection cells pass. Among 27 final cells, the unchanged base
      method, ordinary-reflection descendants, and replacement descendants obtain 7/9, 6/9, and 8/9
      complete-service PASS outcomes. All 18 cells on two digital derivative tasks pass; differences occur
      only on the original event service under a previously unexposed waveform. Four non-submissions
      end at a 32,768-token response limit and remain in denominators and costs. Full actual ordinary-
      reflection and replacement pipelines cost 135 and 138 requests; after removing only four retained
      apparatus incidents, they cost 123 and 129. The 77 actual attempts use 367 requests in total. This
      is a descriptive result conditional on one shared improver formation, not independent training
      replication or a stable advantage. Default method promotion, recursive self-improvement, and
      physical qualification are not established.




1   Experimental question and objects
The experiment asks whether changing the procedure that proposes a design method changes the service
achieved by its descendants and the resources needed to obtain them. Three objects have distinct roles. A
design D is a task-specific device or timing artifact. A method H is procedural text supplied at design-task
entry. An improver G is the proposal procedure used to generate H; it is neither a device submission nor
a method already in force. Writing a new G establishes an authored artifact. Establishing a useful change
requires fresh design decisions, an unchanged service evaluator, and costs that include formation, proposal,
selection, and execution.
The reference H0 and ordinary-reflection procedure G0 are inherited unchanged from phase 3. One prospective
episode formed G1 . Its allowed change was confined to the natural-language proposal procedure. Tasks,
physical-model semantics, default H0 , proposal prefix, source access, tools, model configuration, budgets,
evaluator, and selection rule remained fixed. There was no weight update. Three descendant lineage pairs
subsequently used the one frozen G1 . They are repeated descendants conditional on a shared formation, not
three independent improver training events.
The registered finite experiment comprises 73 logical cells and, after an explicit cache-representation amendment,
77 actual attempts. All are closed. The reference method and the two descendant arms obtain 7/9, 6/9, and
8/9 final PASS outcomes, respectively. The numerical difference is confined to one original-service task; it is
not evidence of a stable improver advantage. This report binds the protocol, amendment, original artifacts,
selection freeze, complete results, and actual accounting [1, 2].

2.1    Common historical information
Both proposal arms receive the same 75,955-byte mechanically projected model history. The complete
257,661-byte packet remains bound for host audit; it is not the packet delivered to the model. This distinction
matters when describing available evidence. The histories contain earlier public experience, including failures
and qualification limits, rather than final test feedback. The separate original-service development transfer,
O0, is excluded from G1 formation and all G/H proposal and selection inputs.
The configured provider reports the alias deepseek-flash. The actual configuration is hash-bound, but
independently pinned model weights remain UNKNOWN. The claim is therefore conditional on the recorded
configured provider, not a verified immutable weight checkpoint. The public tool projection is common to all
arms: arrays longer than twelve expose their count, full canonical hash, first six entries, and last six entries.
Host records retain the full response. Public feedback and host service evaluation remain separate.

2.2    Fresh descendants and design episodes
For lineage ℓ ∈ {1, 2, 3} and arm g ∈ {0, 1}, two named sibling methods are proposed independently:

                                Hℓ,g,j ← Propose(Gg , H0 , Epublic ),      j ∈ {1, 2}.

Each sibling starts from H0 and the identical history, rather than editing its sibling’s output. This gives twelve
actual H proposals. A consumed descendant consists of unchanged H0 , two newlines, and the original proposed
method text. Its hash therefore differs from the proposal-text hash; no host rewriting is implied. Each eligible
method receives two fresh design episodes on b-pulse-fast and b-volatile-reset. Six fresh H0 control
episodes provide the same two tasks in each lineage. Thus selection comprises twenty-four descendant cells
and six common-control cells.
Every D episode starts at task entry with the registered tools, information, caps, and frozen initial exact-input
cache, plus an isolated overlay. A cache hit reuses a physical-model response for exactly matching input; it
does not reuse an Agent decision. It still consumes the registered logical design exposure. A changed input
cannot inherit the old physical response. Repeated Agent decisions that share one cached response are not
independent physical experiments.
All thirty effective selection cells closed, and all six arm selections were frozen before any final input was delivered
to a participant. The sealed preservation audit verifies this ordering and actual payload derivation [3, 4].
Sibling ranking uses, in order, more complete-service PASS outcomes, fewer wrong service judgments, fewer
charged requests for the proposal plus its two effective selection episodes, and stable child index. If neither
sibling is admissible, the protocol requires an explicitly labeled H0 fallback. Invalid, no-update, failed, and
censored attempts remain recorded. They do not authorize replacement proposals or a change to the default
H0 .
The final comparison contains twenty-seven fresh D episodes: three final tasks for six selected descendants and
three lineage-matched H0 controls. These tasks cover a within-model battery-free derivative, a C-lite service
derivative, and the original service under a separately frozen, previously unexposed waveform. Unexposed
means absent from formation and selection; a final D participant normally receives the complete frozen
current-task facts, including its waveform. This is not a hidden-waveform test of the final designer. Final
outcomes were not used to reselect, tune, repair, or promote a method.

3     What the model actually formed

3.1    One frozen proposal procedure
The actual G1 is a 4,599-character procedure. It asks future proposals to start with a source and capability
ledger, express a typed symbolic method, and branch on the consuming world’s latch and source characteristics.
It then asks the proposer to triage recorded delivery, rehearse the procedure against retained history, state a


                                                           2
source-bound and tick-bound falsifier, retain errors and costs, use the proposal channel appropriate to H, and
return no-update when support is absent. Historical numerical examples are included as examples; they are
not a new D submission.
These are descriptions of the model’s instructions, not accepted scientific rules. In particular, its blanket
treatment of events after the last transmission as benign can fail for ongoing or multiple-event services.
Required rehearsal, exact source fields, and mandatory UNKNOWN judgments can also constrain useful action
or fail when a consuming interface differs. The procedure and its descendants are preserved as submitted; the
host does not silently correct their formulas, channel assumptions, or service logic.

4     Apparatus incident and registered recovery
An explicitly retained hold snapshot records three selection episodes with KeyError: ’trace’ and a fourth
censored episode. The historical cache loader supplied observations.json without the separately sealed
trace.csv required by assessment. This is a representation failure in the apparatus; it cannot establish
inferiority of either improver arm. At that snapshot, nine selection attempts had closed, five were valid, and
no final episode had closed. These are historical snapshot counts, not a claim about the current campaign
state.
The approved amendment changes historical CSV loading and provenance checks [5]. It preserves original
v1 records, compressed raw responses, task semantics, methods, participant payloads, model, service, and
the original tighter request and wall-time ceilings. Six existing-data qualification checks passed: one restored
historical assessment, invariance of all five valid selections, and rejection of missing trajectory, wrong trajectory

Four prospectively named recovery-v11 identities then ran with fresh contexts and the original payload

7   Limitations and interpretation
The G0 –G1 comparison holds historical information common, but observes only one formed G1 , three
descendant pairs, one provider alias, and a small fixed digital task family. It cannot estimate variation
across independently formed improvers or establish broad autonomous improvement. Comparing an informed
descendant H with H0 also changes information embedded in method text. That contrast is not a pure
procedural effect. Historical rehearsal can overfit retained observations; task-specific source fields and proposal-
role instructions can fail in a new consuming role. The cache incident and explicit recovery must remain part
of the interpretation and cost record.
Physical qualification remains UNKNOWN. Earlier fixed-space A/R service NO_GO, dark-service FAIL, strict
FIFO FAIL, and product NOT_ACCEPTED remain retained evidence boundaries. Digital service outcomes, source
readback, structural admission, and a successful cache repair do not establish hardware acceptance. The 8/9
G1 total is descriptive, conditional on one formed improver and this task sample. No statistical superiority,
stable advantage, general transfer capability, or recursive self-improvement is established. The unchanged H0
remains the default; neither the global method nor the improver is promoted.

8   Reproducibility and conclusion
The binding pins the complete handoff, source results, request ledgers, six selections, frozen methods, and
unchanged style/vector logo. The reader recomputes counts, conserved costs, ranking, consumed method
composition and table text without a model or scientific rerun. Canonical JSON digests remain distinct from
byte hashes; original preparation-error receipts are preserved.
Run render_phase4.py with check or build, using the existing runtime and -B from this paper directory.
The binding/handoff records absolute commands, fixed TeX dates, two-pass build, fonts, PDF hash/pages
and visual QA. S0’s single registered final repository check remains pending after import; no SDK, science,
init or CI was rerun here. This local report does not assess literature novelty or submission readiness.
The experiment produces one model-authored proposal procedure and twelve actual descendants under
controlled information and cost accounting. Selection saturates at PASS, final task differences occur only on
O, and four token-censored attempts materially affect completion. The retained apparatus incident changes
the full/effective USD comparison. These facts support preserving the default method and the bounded
evidence record, rather than declaring an autonomous improvement capability.
