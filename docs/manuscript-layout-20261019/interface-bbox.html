Current Verdicts and Legal-Intervention Sufficiency in Battery-Free Design Development manuscript 19 October 2026 Abstract We study when an engineering representation supports a current service verdict and when it supports a legal design change. This public development manuscript binds all reported numbers to executable evidence. Agent benefit, format benefit, physical qualification, and autonomous observation discovery are separate untested claims. 1 Problem A fixed task T and world w define a service verdict V (w, d, T ). A representation R(w, d) is current-verdict sufficient on a declared domain if equality of representations implies equality of current verdicts. For a public legal intervention set D, intervention sufficiency requires the same implication for every common legal change δ ∈ D applied to both designs. The second property need not follow from the first. 2 Observation acquisition Observations add facts at measured acquisition and qualification cost. We distinguish selection of developer- defined observations from constructing executable observations. A static sufficient statistic or a complete executable model may provide all necessary information. 3 Implementation The frozen protocol specifies ideal clamped energy and reset-state models, legal D changes, independent implementations, and negative controls.
