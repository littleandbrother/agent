                  Current Verdicts and Legal-Intervention
                    Sufficiency in Battery-Free Design

                                         Development manuscript

                                               19 October 2026




                                                  Abstract
      We study when an engineering representation supports a current service verdict and when it
      supports a legal design change. This public development manuscript binds all reported numbers
      to executable evidence. Agent benefit, format benefit, physical qualification, and autonomous
      observation discovery are separate untested claims.




1     Problem
A fixed task T and world w define a service verdict V (w, d, T ). A representation R(w, d) is current-verdict
sufficient on a declared domain if equality of representations implies equality of current verdicts. For a public
legal intervention set D, intervention sufficiency requires the same implication for every common legal change
δ ∈ D applied to both designs. The second property need not follow from the first.

2     Observation acquisition
Observations add facts at measured acquisition and qualification cost. We distinguish selection of developer-
defined observations from constructing executable observations. A static sufficient statistic or a complete
executable model may provide all necessary information.

3     Implementation
The frozen protocol specifies ideal clamped energy and reset-state models, legal D changes, independent
implementations, and negative controls. It changes no existing physical or task semantics.

4     E2–E5: public development results

4.3   E5-1: measured finite observation selection
The selector receives researcher-supplied required-field metadata, not outcomes, and chooses the smallest
eligible source by actual file-size estimate. Table 2 totals four public cases. Bytes are payload bytes returned
to Python, separately counting source readback for audit; they are not storage-device I/O or monetary fees.
Wall time includes acquisition, source audit, and selector where used. Source-export setup is retained in the
raw data. These are single host measurements with uncontrolled OS cache, not a latency benchmark.
Fixed-rich, complete raw information, and an executable dynamic response all judge four of four correctly.
The selected observation reads exactly the same facts and bytes as the fixed minimal mechanism-spec baseline.
This batch supplies no independent algorithmic selection gain and does not weaken those strong baselines. A
zero-byte acquisition budget abstains; three illegal changes and a tampered source binding are rejected.

4.4    E5-2: executable construction preparation
A deterministic compiler turns a researcher-specified headroom expression into a new standalone Python
observation, with source hash, input schema and applicability domain. Its values −14 and 8 µJ agree with
independent integration on all 20 exposed clamp-grid points and in two fresh-process executions. Construction
took 0.239 ms; reference qualification took 13.017 ms (20 model runs); the two fresh processes took 37.730 ms
combined. The equation and relevant inputs were manually supplied. This validates executable construction
and audit machinery, not autonomous discovery or held-out usefulness.

5     E4: information and format
Free-text and structured packets share exact facts and summary at each information level. Fixed-information
trials prohibit additional reads; active-observation trials share the same initial packet and legal tools. Deter-
ministic parsing is a data integrity check; model-format effects are not measured here.

6     E5: selection and construction
The first stage uses a declared finite observation catalogue. The second compiles a researcher-specified
expression into a new executable and checks it against an independent reference. Human-supplied formulas
are not Agent discoveries.

7     Boundaries and threats
All examples are exposed public development mechanisms. Two implementations of the same ideal equations
check implementation consistency, not device reality. Fixed-rich, full raw information, executable dynamic
models, and sufficient static controls remain valid baselines. No novelty priority is claimed without a literature
audit. Historical three-family refinement tied the fixed-rich baseline and remains a separate development
result.
