# M2: feedback consumption did not change the observed engineering actions

The bounded package is closed. In the four executed deterministic tracks, full M1 Update consumption (U) selected exactly the same four actions and obtained the same original-consumer outcomes as the feedback-disabled ablation (F). The startup/TX mechanism passed with the first selected candidate in both. The gateway-recovery mechanism remained a valid-domain service failure after three evaluated candidates in each. U spent eight additional cheap scores on the second mechanism without changing any selected action. These observations do not establish a feedback benefit.

The strong-generic comparison is incomplete: its first track used the common predictor but was interrupted by a local request-body limit before a candidate was submitted; the second G track was not executed after the original bridge conservatively treated that transport refusal as missing usage. Neither is a scientific loss or evidence that G is inferior. All affected cells, original errors and costs remain; no replacement, rescue or rerun was made.
