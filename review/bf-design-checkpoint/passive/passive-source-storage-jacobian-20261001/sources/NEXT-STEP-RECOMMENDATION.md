# Smallest useful next step after the static review

## Recommendation

Prepare one bounded, separately versioned passive source/storage comparison, rather than more detached static examples or a native-feasibility claim. The current ROM and generalized-force code are sufficient building blocks for that conditional mathematical experiment. They are not yet an admitted dynamic provider. Missing measured material and native PMIC data limit its claim; they do not make a clearly declared model-sensitivity question intrinsically uninformative.

The useful question is narrow: under one fixed conditional mechanical/material/loss model and the unchanged source geometry and prescribed door motion, how does changing only input storage affect the coupled mechanical/electrical trajectory and energy partition?

## A. Conditional passive source/storage sensitivity

Before any execution, freeze a connected runner and exact protocol:

1. Use the new signed q/voltage port and generalized magnetic force/potential together. Reference the canonical geometry/input provenance but assign a distinct conditional model/study identity; do not relabel this as the native LTC3588 world.
2. Specify a complete passive circuit with no PMIC, firmware or receiver. An explicitly ideal passive rectifier/storage benchmark is defensible; an ideal circuit is not automatically an upper bound on a real feedback-coupled device. No invented LTC3588 currents, startup law or auxiliary readiness state.
3. Declare damping. The source's zeta=0.02 may be retained as a tagged development assumption, not a measured value. It cannot become a robustness claim. Cp_Ritz remains the capacitance of the chosen one-mode branch; do not silently substitute endpoint-static or stress-free-material capacitance.
4. Either justify the q-only mechanical truncation to the required numerical/sensitivity resolution or explicitly narrow the result to that assumed path. The available alpha/beta formulas help bound omitted axial shift once actual force/voltage/gradient bounds exist. An interval-wide derivative bound and invariant interval are needed for the feedback contraction argument; a few point derivatives do not suffice. General torque can also excite discarded bending shapes.
5. Bind one stable, in-domain closed-door equilibrium for the new model before the two designs, with a predeclared root-selection rule and failure outcome if no suitable state is found. Include initial elastic and mutual magnetic potential energy. Zero piezo voltage implies Q0=theta*q0, not Q0=0 when q0 is nonzero. Physical preparation/reset remains unqualified.
6. Close the total energy ledger: kinetic + beam elastic + piezo + all passive electrical stores + mutual magnetic potential. Prescribed-driver work must include its translational and rotational magnetic power. Track dissipation separately; do not treat initial magnetic/mechanical energy as unexplained electrical harvesting.
7. Predeclare numerical, force-quadrature, energy and domain checks, execution budgets, output fields and stop conditions. Check the actually visited path, not just these five static states. Magnet contact, model-domain exit, nonfinite output or unresolved numerical refinement means stop/insufficient evidence, not a physical design failure. No old transverse-only table may be reused under the new force identity.

Then a minimal matched design is the already proposed baseline/storage-only intervention (47 versus 10 microfarads), each at two predeclared solver-resolution settings: four trajectories, no adaptive search or extra design selection. Step sizes should follow the connected passive circuit's stiffness/event requirements and a bounded cost plan, rather than inherit PMIC sampling settings blindly. Freeze the comparison and criteria before running. A zero-motion equilibrium residual and passive-circuit analytic controls can be software checks rather than an expanded design campaign.

A successful result qualifies only this conditional numerical source/storage comparison. Report trajectory/energy effects and failures; do not generate startup, packet, product, hardware or feasible-reference flags. If the connected runner cannot pass those checks, preserve that failure and stop this branch pending a specifically justified change. This prevents both premature admission and an indefinite sequence of detached diagnostics.
